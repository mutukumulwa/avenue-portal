## D. Your services and prices

This is what makes the trial meaningful. Without it we can show you screens; with it we can
show you what Medvex would have paid on your actual bills.

**1. Your service list, exported from your system.** Whatever your hospital system or
billing book calls it — a service master, price list, or tariff sheet. We need three columns
at minimum:

| Your service code | Your description | Your price (UGX) |
|---|---|---|
| e.g. SER015 | Consultation — General Practitioner | 30,000 |

Keeping **your own service codes** matters more than it sounds. They become the link
between what you bill and what we pay, so reconciliation is automatic instead of manual.

**2. For each service, tell us where it applies:**

- Unit — per item, per day, per visit, per session
- Does it need approval before you provide it?
- Any limit — maximum units per visit, maximum times per year
- Any restriction — adults only, women only, and so on

## E. Your real operations — what we test against

You do not have to identify any patient. Please remove names, and replace patient numbers
with sequential numbers, before sending anything in this section.

**1. A sample of your recent bills — ideally 20 to 50.** A mix of outpatient and at least a
few inpatient admissions with the itemised bill and the length of stay. We replay these
through Medvex and show you, line by line, what would have been paid, what would have been
queried, and why. This is the single most useful thing you can send.

**2. Your top 20 services by volume**, and **your top 20 diagnoses by volume**, for the last
six to twelve months. This tells us whether the trial is covering the work you actually do
or just the work that is easy to demonstrate.

**3. Where it hurts today** — please answer honestly, including the awkward parts:

| Question | Your answer |
|---|---|
| Which insurers or schemes do you currently serve? | |
| From submitting a claim, how many days until you are actually paid? | |
| Roughly what share of your claims get rejected or short-paid? | |
| What are the three most common reasons given? | |
| Who at Family Hospital chases rejections, and how much of their week does it take? | |
| How do you check today whether a patient is covered before you treat them? | |

Without your current numbers we have no baseline, and "faster" becomes an opinion rather
than something we can prove to you.

## What happens next

1. You return sections A–C. We create Family Hospital and issue logins — typically same day.
2. Your team signs in and works through eligibility checks, pre-authorisation and claim
   submission against a test scheme, with our support on a call.
3. You return sections D–E. We load your price list and replay your own bills.
4. We sit down together over the results, including anything that did not work.

**Questions on any of this — reply to this document and ask.** An item you are unsure about
is better raised than guessed.
